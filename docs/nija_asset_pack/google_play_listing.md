```text
Your Digital Life. Protected.

Store, organize, and protect your most sensitive personal information with Nija – Private Digital Vault, a local-first encrypted personal information management app built for Android.

Unlike cloud-dependent managers that require accounts before you can store a single item, Nija keeps your data on your device by default. Create a private vault in seconds, protect it with your master password, and stay in complete control of how and where your information is backed up.

EVERYTHING IMPORTANT. ONE PLACE.
Nija goes beyond a traditional password manager so you can keep all confidential records organized inside a single encrypted vault:
• Passwords & Login Credentials: Save usernames, passwords, websites, and notes with quick copy and masking controls.
• Identity Documents: Store passport details, ID numbers, and expiry dates cleanly.
• Payment Cards: Keep debit and credit card details accessible when you need them.
• PDFs & Important Documents: Attach and store PDFs and personal files directly inside your vault.
• Personal Notes: Keep private notes, recovery codes, and confidential records protected.
• Wi-Fi & Server Credentials: Organize network passwords, SSH details, and infrastructure credentials alongside everyday logins.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Local-First Architecture: Your vault data stays on your device by default.
• Encrypted Storage: Information inside your vault is protected with Argon2id key derivation and AES-256-GCM encryption.
• No Account Required: Start using Nija immediately without registering an email address or creating an online profile.
• Optional Cloud Backup: Google Drive backup is strictly optional—never mandatory.
• Portable .nija Vault Files: Create a new local vault or open an existing encrypted .nija vault file directly from your device.

SECURITY IN YOUR HANDS
• Master Password Protection: Unlock your vault with a master password that only you know.
• Biometric Unlock & App PIN: Use fingerprint or face unlock, or set a quick App PIN for convenient access.
• Automatic Locking: Configure auto-lock timers to lock Nija automatically when idle.
• Recovery Phrase & Local Backups: Back up your recovery phrase offline and maintain rolling local backup snapshots on your device.

EFFORTLESS ORGANIZATION
• Folders & Nested Structure: Group items across Personal, Work, Finance, Travel, or custom folders.
• Categories, Tags & Favorites: Filter instantly by item type, apply custom tags, and star frequently used entries.
• Fast Vault Search: Search across stored logins, notes, cards, identities, and documents in seconds.

Take control of your personal information with a private digital vault designed around local ownership and clarity.
```
